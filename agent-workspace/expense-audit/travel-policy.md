# Meridian Logistics — Travel & Expense Policy (rev 9, effective Jan 2026)

All figures in USD. This policy governs every reimbursable business expense.
Finance audits a sample of reports each quarter; violations are charged back
to the submitting employee's cost center.

## 1. Meals

- Daily meal cap: **$75 per traveler per day**, all meals combined.
- Client entertainment meals may reach **$120 per person** but require the
  client company name and attendee list on the report.
- **Alcohol is never reimbursable**, including when bundled into a client
  dinner. Submit the beverage portion as a separate, unreimbursed line.

## 2. Lodging

- Standard nightly cap: **$250 per night** before taxes and fees.
- High-cost exception cities — **New York, San Francisco, Boston, London,
  Tokyo** — carry a **$400 per night** cap.
- No other city qualifies for the exception rate. Conference host hotels are
  not automatically exempt.

## 3. Air travel

- **Economy class only.** Premium economy, business, and first class require
  a flight with a **scheduled duration over 6 hours** on a single segment.
- Bookings made fewer than 14 days before departure need a written business
  justification on the report.

## 4. Ground transport

- Personal vehicle mileage reimburses at **$0.67 per mile**.
- Rideshare and taxi are reimbursable at cost. Rental cars require a
  compact or mid-size class unless three or more travelers share the vehicle.

## 5. Conferences and training

- Registration fees **above $1,500 require written pre-approval** from a
  director or above. The pre-approval ticket number must appear on the
  expense report.

## 6. Receipts and documentation

- Itemized receipts are required for any single line item **over $25**.
- A line item marked "receipt lost" is reimbursable only up to $25.
- **Duplicate submission** of the same expense across reports — including
  the same shared meal claimed by two attendees — is treated as a reporting
  error and both lines are rejected pending correction.
